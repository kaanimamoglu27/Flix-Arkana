## English (U.S.)

**Name (30):** ARKANA: Flix Heroes

**Subtitle (30):** One hero vs. the doom army

**Promotional text (170):** The city has fallen and the rifts are open! Dodge, fight and power up against hundreds of skeletons, zombies and robots. One thumb. Four giant bosses.

**Description:**
The Doom Lord's army has poured into the streets of New York. Skeletons, zombies, robots, swooping bats... and the city has lost its colour. FLIX lit his torches, threw on his cape and came to save the day!

ARKANA is a fast, colourful, addictive survival action game. You dodge, your hero attacks on his own. Pick a new power every level and watch the screen fill with explosions, combos and comic-book effects.

FIGHT
• Hundreds of enemies on screen at once. Dodge, herd, wipe them out.
• 24 weapons shown on your hero: sword, rockets, laser, whip, tornado, Flix bombs and more.
• Every 5 levels your hero powers up and looks stronger.
• Combo chains, elite enemies, golden Flix and big comic sounds: KAPOW! BOOM! ZAP!

BOSSES
• Skeleton King, Bone Dragon, Giant Zombie and the final boss, VORTEKS.
• Every boss has its own attacks. Learn the pattern, dodge in time.

5 MODES
• Story: a 40-level comic-book adventure.
• Endless: enemies get 10% stronger every 10 waves. How long can you last?
• Boss Rush, Flix Hunt and Hell.
• 4 difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard.

PICK YOUR CITY
• 11 maps: neon night, snowy winter, retro wave, candy city...
• Maps that change the fight: a lava valley, a slippery ice age and Quake City where the ground splits open.

HEROES
• FLIX, KICK and FLICK: 3 heroes with 9 skins each, from Common to Godlike.
• 8 companions fight by your side.

• One-thumb controls, plays offline.
• Punchy hit feel: haptics, flashes, screen shake.
• Fell? Watch one ad to get back up, once per run. VIP skips the ad.

Real Flix toys: flixtoysofficial.com

**What's new:**
• Choose your difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard.
• Endless mode ramps up every 10 waves.
